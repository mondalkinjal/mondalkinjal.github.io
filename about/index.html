About

I am a PhD candidate in Biophysics at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Jeffery B. Klauda, Antonio Cardone, and Ella Mihailescu. My doctoral research combines molecular simulations and machine learning to generate new proteins and predict their functions. A secondary aim of my research is to develop machine learning methods for analysing and enhancing molecular simulations.

In my undergraduate career, I developed theoretical biophysics models for proteins searching for their targets on the DNA as a researcher in Srabanti Chaudhury’s group at IISER Pune, where I modeled how proteins find their targets on DNA.
